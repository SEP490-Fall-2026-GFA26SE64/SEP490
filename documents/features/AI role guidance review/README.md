## REQ-049-F01: Review AI Target Role Guidance

**Description:** Review suggested roles/comments using Employee baseline and RIASEC results; confirm, revise, or reject the guidance.
**Input:** Authorized Employee review, assessment evidence, generated suggestions/comments, and Expert decision/revisions.
**Outcome:** Recorded review outcome; reviewed guidance can support Employee role selection/confirmation. Expert review does not itself select the Employee's role.
**Acceptance:** An authorized Expert can inspect the evidence and perform each of confirm/revise/reject. Unauthorized Experts cannot view or change the review. Unreviewed output cannot be represented as approved.

**Open decisions:** Reviewer allocation and discipline/role boundaries, required reasons, concurrent reviewers, revision/rejection cycles, publication of outcomes, and notifications.
**Entities/design gaps:** Guidance and review history; old AI soft-skill comment records are not silently migrated into this workflow.
