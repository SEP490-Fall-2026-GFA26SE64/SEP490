# View Platform Organization Statistics

## Current Scope Alignment
Retired platform/multi-organization scope; follow the [disposition](README.md#current-scope-alignment). L&D learning monitoring is separately REQ-025/026.

## Historical Specification
The material below is not a current implementation requirement.

**Function ID:** REQ-031-F01  
**Actor:** Platform Administrator  
**Status:** Pending clarification  
**Feature:** [Organization Statistics](README.md)

## Description

View counts and aggregate usage of platform organizations, including subscription status. This is not unrestricted access to private employee answers or expert comments.

**Input:** Organization/subscription records and permitted usage aggregates.  
**Outcome:** Platform organization statistics.

## Open Decisions

- Active-organization definition, required metrics, and reporting periods.
- Per-organization drill-down, personally identifiable information limits, and minimum aggregation sizes.
- Refresh interval and inclusion of demo, benchmark, or cancelled organizations.

## Acceptance Baseline

Platform reporting does not silently grant employee-level assessment access.
