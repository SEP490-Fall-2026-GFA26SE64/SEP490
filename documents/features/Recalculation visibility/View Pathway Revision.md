# View Pathway Revision

## Current Scope Alignment
Employee views remaining-pathway revisions/explanations driven by new evidence and learning needs, not only failed questions. Follow the [current feature contract](README.md#current-scope-alignment) and [System Overview](../../adapt_learn_graph_system_overview.md).

## Historical Specification
Conflicting actors, policies and acceptance below are superseded; old evidence is not revised-scope certification.

**Function ID:** REQ-010-F01  
**Actor:** Employee  
**Status:** Pending clarification  
**Feature:** [Recalculation Visibility](README.md)

## Description

Show the revised pathway and explain its automatic changes using failed-question evidence and the identified skill gap.

**Input:** Employee pathway revision and its trigger evidence.  
**Outcome:** Visible revised modules and an explanation of why the pathway changed.

## Open Decisions

- Latest revision only or complete history; side-by-side changes or a summary.
- Whether acknowledgement is required and how expert adjustments are distinguished.
- Presentation while recalculation is running or has failed.

## Acceptance Baseline

An automatic revision is linked to its gap rationale and is not presented as an employee-requested module change.
