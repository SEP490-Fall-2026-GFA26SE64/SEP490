# View Organization Target Roles

**Function ID:** REQ-023-F02  
**Actor:** Organization Administrator  
**Status:** Pending clarification  
**Feature:** [Target Role Management](README.md)

## Description

Browse and inspect the organization's target-role catalog for administrative management.

**Input:** Organization context and role filters.  
**Outcome:** Authorized target-role list and details.

## Open Decisions

- List columns, search, filters, and sorting.
- Display of assigned experts, employee counts, and graph readiness.
- Visibility of draft, inactive, or archived role versions.

## Acceptance Baseline

The administrator can inspect own-organization roles without receiving expert graph-editing permissions automatically.
