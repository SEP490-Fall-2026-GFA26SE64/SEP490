# Update Target Role

**Function ID:** REQ-023-F03  
**Actor:** Organization Administrator  
**Status:** Pending clarification  
**Feature:** [Target Role Management](README.md)

## Description

Update target-role administrative metadata. Changes to micro-skill requirements remain the responsibility of assigned experts.

**Input:** Organization target role and edited metadata.  
**Outcome:** Updated role details under the agreed lifecycle policy.

## Open Decisions

- Editable fields and permission to publish, unpublish, or deactivate.
- Role versioning and changes while employees have active pathways.
- Whether a renamed or reclassified role requires employee/expert notification.

## Acceptance Baseline

Administrative metadata changes do not silently modify the role's skill requirements or erase existing role selections.
