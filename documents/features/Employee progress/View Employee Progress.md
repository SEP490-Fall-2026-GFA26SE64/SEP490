# View Employee Progress

## Current Scope Alignment
L&D Manager monitors employee profiles, gaps, assessment results and progress, including independent skill learning. Follow the [current feature contract](README.md#current-scope-alignment) and [System Overview](../../adapt_learn_graph_system_overview.md); detailed record access requires privacy agreement.

## Historical Specification
Conflicting actors, policies and acceptance below are superseded; old evidence is not revised-scope certification.

**Function ID:** REQ-025-F01  
**Actor:** Organization Administrator  
**Status:** Pending clarification  
**Feature:** [Employee Progress](README.md)

## Description

View organization employees' selected roles, assessment completion, pathway progress, and competency development.

**Input:** Organization scope, selected employee, and any reporting filters.  
**Outcome:** An authorized employee progress summary and agreed drill-down details.

## Open Decisions

- Progress formula, freshness, comparison period, and status labels.
- Whether detailed answers and expert/AI soft-skill comments are visible.
- Filtering by target role or other organization groups and historical role changes.

## Acceptance Baseline

The displayed progress belongs to the administrator's organization and does not equate module viewing alone with skill mastery.
