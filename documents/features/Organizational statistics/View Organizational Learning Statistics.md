# View Organizational Learning Statistics

**Function ID:** REQ-026-F01  
**Actor:** Organization Administrator  
**Status:** Pending clarification  
**Feature:** [Organizational Statistics](README.md)

## Description

Display organization-level learning, skill, and role statistics, including agreed measures of completion, gaps, and progress.

**Input:** Organization records and selected reporting scope.  
**Outcome:** Aggregated organization statistics with defined metric meanings.

## Open Decisions

- Required metrics and exact numerator/denominator definitions.
- Date filters, cohort grouping, comparisons, and drill-down permissions.
- Refresh interval, unknown/unassessed skills, and inactive-user inclusion.

## Acceptance Baseline

Aggregates include only authorized organization data and distinguish missing evidence from demonstrated skill gaps.
