# Complete Soft Skill Assessment

**Function ID:** REQ-003-F01  
**Actor:** Employee  
**Status:** Pending clarification  
**Feature:** [Soft Skill Assessment](README.md)

## Description

Complete the configured soft-skill assessment and retain responses for target-role matching and expert-reviewed AI comments. This does not introduce a separate 360-degree review workflow.

**Input:** Employee, target role, assessment items, and responses.  
**Outcome:** Recorded soft-skill assessment evidence and its available result.

## Related Policy Requiring Revalidation

- In [Choose Target Role](../Target%20role%20selection/Choose%20Target%20Role.md) (REQ-001-F02), 2026-09-26, the user selected reuse of existing soft-skill results after a role change, with new AI comments relative to the new role, but explicitly requested revalidation.
- Clarified on 2026-09-26: Revalidation means revisiting the product decision later, not operational checks on reused results. The reuse policy remains provisional and deferred in Choose Target Role; it does not establish an additional approval step or validity period.

## Confirmed Related Rule

- From the role-readiness interview, 2026-09-27, option A: If the selected role loses baseline readiness, soft-skill assessment remains independently available for eligible employees. The assessment continues subject to its own configuration, authorization, and completion rules; baseline readiness loss alone does not block a soft-skill assessment start or in-progress completion. This decision does not grant duplicate or cross-role soft-skill reuse, and it does not change the separate baseline-start or evidence-only rules.
- Decision log, 2026-09-27: Should baseline readiness loss block soft-skill assessments for an already-selected role? Option A: Allow starting and continuing an otherwise eligible soft-skill assessment regardless of baseline readiness; use the assessment's own eligibility rules. This is independent from baseline-start and evidence-only closure rules.

## Open Decisions

- Questionnaire, situational judgment, free text, or a combination; soft-skill dimensions and scoring rubric.
- Required versus optional completion, timing, retakes, and answer visibility.
- Relationship to the technical pathway and what employees see before expert review.

## Acceptance Baseline

Responses are associated with the correct employee and target role and are available only to authorized downstream evaluation and review functions.

- An employee can continue or start an otherwise eligible soft-skill assessment while the selected role is baseline-unready, provided the assessment's own rules allow the action. Baseline readiness loss alone is not a soft-skill blocker.
