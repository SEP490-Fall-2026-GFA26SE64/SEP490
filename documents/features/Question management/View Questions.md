## Description

Browse authorized questions and inspect their content, scoring information, and available mappings.

**Input:** Expert permissions and optional question filters.  
**Outcome:** An authorized question list and detail view.

## Open Decisions

- Search, skill/module/type filters, and pagination.
- Whether draft/archived questions and usage statistics are visible.
- Assessment preview and protection of answer keys from employee-facing views.

## Acceptance Baseline

Expert question access is separate from an employee's permitted assessment/result access.
