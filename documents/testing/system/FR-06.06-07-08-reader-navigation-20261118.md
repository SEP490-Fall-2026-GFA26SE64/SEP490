# FR-06.06 / FR-06.07 / FR-06.08 — Reader Context-Aware Return Navigation & Skill Assessment Separation, 18 November 2026

## Scope and results

- **Context-aware Reader return navigation (`AC-01`, `AC-02`)**: Opening a module from Upskilling (`/employee/upskilling?tab=skills`) appends `?from=upskilling&skillId=<id>&level=<level>` to `/employee/modules/:moduleId`. `LearningReaderHeader` and `LearningModuleReaderPage` render `Back to Skill` / `Quay lại kỹ năng` (or `Back to Upskilling` when skill context is absent) and navigate back to `/employee/upskilling?tab=skills&skillId=<id>&level=<level>`, where `UpskillSkillsPanel` automatically restores the selected Skill and Knowledge Level tab. Opening a module from Pathway (or without `from=upskilling`) retains `Back to Pathway` / `Về lộ trình` (`/employee/pathway`).
- **Clean Reader completion state without inline assessment clutter (`AC-03`, `AC-04`)**: Removed inline `CompletedModuleAssessmentLink` (`Take Skill Assessment` / `Assessment history`) and `SkillLearningPanel` from the bottom of `LearningModuleReaderPage`. Completing all module lessons displays a clean completion banner with a CTA button (`Back to Skill` or `Back to Pathway`) that flushes progress and returns the learner to the parent Skill or Pathway surface where `Skill Assessment` is managed.

## Manual checklist

1. Sign in as an Employee on `http://localhost:8088`; open **Upskilling → Skills → View modules** on an active Skill and choose a knowledge level tab (`Foundation`, `Intermediate`, or `Advanced`).
2. Click **Start learning**, **Continue learning**, or **Review module** on an eligible module.
3. Verify the URL includes `?from=upskilling&skillId=...&level=...` and the top-left Reader header displays **Back to Skill** (`Quay lại kỹ năng` in Vietnamese) instead of **Back to Pathway**.
4. Reload (F5) the Reader page and confirm **Back to Skill** remains intact.
5. Complete all lessons in the module and verify the bottom of the Reader shows the **Module content completed** banner with a **Back to Skill** CTA button, and does **not** render the inline **Take Skill Assessment** / **Assessment history** block or optional-module list at the bottom of the lesson.
6. Click **Back to Skill** (either in the header or in the completion banner) and verify the browser returns directly to `/employee/upskilling?tab=skills&skillId=...&level=...` with that Skill and knowledge-level tab open and the **Skill Assessment** section visible at the top.
7. Open a module from **My Learning / Pathway** (`/employee/pathway`) and verify the Reader header and completion banner display **Back to Pathway** (`Về lộ trình`) and return to `/employee/pathway`.
