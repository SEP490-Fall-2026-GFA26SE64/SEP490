## Assigned tasks and submission evidence


In Data modelling and storage, demonstrate the following senior tasks:

- MS-DE01: Compare cross-domain model alternatives; justify performance and change-impact trade-offs and review another design.
- MS-DE03: Compare warehouse structures and loading strategies under scale and change; justify the selected design.
- MS-DE06: Review metadata coverage across domains; resolve conflicting definitions and propose ownership controls.

Submit evidence:

- MS-DE01: Model diagram, schema, constraint checks and design rationale.
- MS-DE03: Warehouse schema, load scripts, history tests and reconciliation results.
- MS-DE06: Data dictionary, ownership records, change history and review findings.
