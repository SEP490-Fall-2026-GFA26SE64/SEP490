## Focused practice

Choose the practice appropriate to the selected Level. These are retained local proposals, not official framework scoring standards.

### Practice adapted from MOD-DE-J-QUAL-02

- Profile a supplied dataset for missing values, duplicates and distributions using a checklist.
- Profiling output, anomaly examples, risk assessment and sampling rationale.

### Practice adapted from MOD-DE-M-QUAL-02

- Profile multiple sources independently; identify anomalous segments and quantify ingestion risks.
- Profiling output, anomaly examples, risk assessment and sampling rationale.

### Practice adapted from MOD-DE-S-QUAL-02

- Design a profiling approach for changing sources; prioritise risks and validate whether sampling misses important defects.
- Profiling output, anomaly examples, risk assessment and sampling rationale.
