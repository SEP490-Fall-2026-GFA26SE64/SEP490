## Focused practice

Choose the practice appropriate to the selected Level. These are retained local proposals, not official framework scoring standards.

### Practice adapted from MOD-DE-J-QUAL-01

- Run supplied completeness, accuracy and consistency checks; record failures with guidance.
- Quality rules, failing records, investigation notes and rerun results.

### Practice adapted from MOD-DE-M-QUAL-01

- Define dataset-specific quality checks, investigate failures and verify corrections independently.
- Quality rules, failing records, investigation notes and rerun results.

### Practice adapted from MOD-DE-S-QUAL-01

- Design quality controls across a data solution; evaluate false alarms, missed failures and ownership.
- Quality rules, failing records, investigation notes and rerun results.
