## Focused practice

Choose the practice appropriate to the selected Level. These are retained local proposals, not official framework scoring standards.

### Practice adapted from MOD-QA-J-AUTO-02

- Rerun an inconsistent test under supplied controls and record failure patterns with guidance.
- Rerun logs, controlled experiments, cause analysis and post-fix results.

### Practice adapted from MOD-QA-M-AUTO-02

- Diagnose flaky tests independently by controlling data, timing and environment; verify a correction.
- Rerun logs, controlled experiments, cause analysis and post-fix results.

### Practice adapted from MOD-QA-S-AUTO-02

- Review flakiness across a suite; prioritise causes and verify that reliability improvements preserve fault detection.
- Rerun logs, controlled experiments, cause analysis and post-fix results.
