# Assess technical vulnerabilities

Module ID: MOD-READ-SEC06. Content version: 1.1. Review: NEEDS_EXPERT_REVIEW.

## Guided reading

Read the official resource [Vulnerability assessment](https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9/skills/vulnerability-assessment). Locate the cited capability and explain the behaviours and constraints described there in your own words. The resource provides professional scope, not a complete tool-specific tutorial.

## Learning outcome

Identify and validate findings in an authorized assessment scope and explain their impact.

## Local practice

Choose a small synthetic system or the sandbox supplied by the organization. State its inputs, intended outputs, constraints and stakeholders. Demonstrate the outcome above with an artifact appropriate to the action (for example a script, configuration, model, change record or reasoned decision). Keep an expected-versus-observed check and explain one limitation. Do not perform exercises against production systems.

## Evidence and Level context

Use the selected Level's REQUIRES_SKILL.expectedOutcome and rubric 1.2 to select the actual assessment task. Junior uses supplied procedures and review; Middle resolves a defined task independently; Senior justifies complex decisions and reviews their impact. Submit the artifact, checks, assumptions and a brief explanation of your own decisions. Reading or completing this module alone does not prove skill mastery.
