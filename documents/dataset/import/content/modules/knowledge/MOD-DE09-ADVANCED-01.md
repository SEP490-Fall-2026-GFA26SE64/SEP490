# Profile source datasets — Guided practice

## Learning objective

Practice **Profile source datasets** at advanced level. This is pre-published development sample content; it is not a human-reviewed course.

## Scenario and task

Profile a small orders dataset. Record field types, missing values, duplicates and unexpected values; explain which findings prevent safe downstream use.

## Level-specific practice

Evaluate competing approaches under explicit constraints. Address recovery and edge cases, and justify your decisions with scoped observations.

## Evidence to keep

Keep the small learning artifact, expected-versus-observed checks and a short explanation of your decisions. Use synthetic inputs and record any assumptions.
