# Improve existing software structure — Guided practice

## Learning objective

Practice **Improve existing software structure** at foundation level. This is pre-published development sample content; it is not a human-reviewed course.

## Scenario and task

Refactor a small order-total calculation into clear responsibilities. Preserve its input/output behavior and document how you checked representative cases.

## Level-specific practice

Follow one worked example, make one controlled change and explain the expected result.

## Evidence to keep

Keep the small learning artifact, expected-versus-observed checks and a short explanation of your decisions. Use synthetic inputs and record any assumptions.

## Completion and assessment

Complete the lesson, then take the configured Skill assessment for MS_FS15_FOUNDATION. Reading or marking the module complete does not award official achievement; the assessment and applicable prerequisite rules still apply.
