## Assigned tasks and submission evidence


In Model development and evaluation, demonstrate the following senior tasks:

- MS-AI04: Compare method families for a complex task; justify assumptions, adoption risks and limits of the recommendation.
- MS-AI05: Review model alternatives under operational constraints; justify the selected model and document failure limits.
- MS-AI06: Design evaluation for an adoption decision; analyse segment failures, uncertainty and metric trade-offs.

Submit evidence:

- MS-AI04: Task formulation, candidate methods, baseline results and method rationale.
- MS-AI05: Training code, model artifacts, experiment records and model comparison.
- MS-AI06: Evaluation protocol, metric results, segment analysis and decision rationale.
