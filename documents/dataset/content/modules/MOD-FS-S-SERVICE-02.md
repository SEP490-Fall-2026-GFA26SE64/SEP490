## Assigned tasks and submission evidence


In Application services and data, demonstrate the following senior tasks:

- MS-FS12: Review validation across trust boundaries; justify shared rules and verify bypass and inconsistency scenarios.
- MS-FS13: Review authorization design across services; justify policy decisions and verify high-impact bypass scenarios.
- MS-FS14: Compare transaction strategies across complex interactions; justify consistency and recovery trade-offs.

Submit evidence:

- MS-FS12: Validation rules, client/server code and boundary/bypass test results.
- MS-FS13: Permission matrix, authorization checks and allow/deny/bypass tests.
- MS-FS14: Transaction code, boundary rationale and rollback/concurrency results.
