## Assigned tasks and submission evidence


In Data modelling and storage, demonstrate the following middle tasks:

- MS-DE01: Independently design a model for multiple consumers; validate constraints and explain normalisation choices.
- MS-DE03: Design warehouse grain and incremental loads; validate history handling and reconciliation independently.
- MS-DE06: Maintain metadata through schema changes; verify definitions with owners and dependent consumers.

Submit evidence:

- MS-DE01: Model diagram, schema, constraint checks and design rationale.
- MS-DE03: Warehouse schema, load scripts, history tests and reconciliation results.
- MS-DE06: Data dictionary, ownership records, change history and review findings.
