## Assigned tasks and submission evidence


In Model development and evaluation, demonstrate the following senior tasks:

- MS-AI10: Design and review validation for grouped or temporal data; assess variability and selection bias.
- MS-AI11: Compare search strategies under compute constraints; justify gains, stability and avoidance of test-set overfitting.
- MS-AI13: Compare failure patterns across candidate models; prioritise mitigations and state unresolved limitations.

Submit evidence:

- MS-AI10: Fold definitions, preprocessing boundaries, fold results and final-test access record.
- MS-AI11: Search configuration, budget, trial log and selection rationale.
- MS-AI13: Error analysis, segmented results, example failures and mitigation checks.
